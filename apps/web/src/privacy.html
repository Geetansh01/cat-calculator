Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers the CAT On Screen Calculator website and the "CAT On-Screen Calculator - Floating Overlay" browser extension. The calculator is an independent practice tool and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the IIMs or the official CAT exam.

Browser extension

The extension collects, stores, and transmits no personal data. Everything you type into the calculator is processed on your device and is never sent anywhere. The extension makes no network requests.

The only thing it saves is the on-screen position of the calculator window, in your browser's local extension storage, so it reopens where you left it. This never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

The extension runs only on a tab where you click its toolbar button (or use its keyboard shortcut). It does not read, record, or modify the content of the pages you visit.

Website

The website uses Google Analytics to count visits and understand which features are used (for example, page views and clicks on the extension link). Google Analytics may set cookies and collect anonymised usage data such as approximate location, device and browser type. Calculator input is never sent to us or to Google. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

If you dismiss the extension suggestion banner, that choice is remembered in your browser's local storage on your device.
